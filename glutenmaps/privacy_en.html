Glutenmaps Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Glutenmaps is an iOS app that shows gluten-free places nearby. It is developed in his spare time by Anton Wubben. This policy describes what data the app uses and what happens to it.

Summary

  • Glutenmaps has no account and collects no personal data.
  • Your location is only used while the app is open, to show places nearby. It is not stored on any server of the developer.
  • To fetch places, the coordinates of the search area are sent to our places database and to public OpenStreetMap servers.
  • Saved places and history are stored only on your own device.
  • No analytics or tracking services are used.
  • The “Search reviews” button opens Google in your browser, with the name, town and country of the place. The app itself sends nothing to Google.

Location

The app asks for permission to use your location, only while the app is open. It uses it to show the map and the places nearby. You can also use the app without permission: just search for a town or address. You can change the permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Fetching places

To show places, the app sends the coordinates and radius of the area you are viewing to the app's database (Supabase) or, outside the area that database covers, to public Overpass servers of OpenStreetMap. For the places file the app also fetches data from antonwubben.nl. As with any internet request, these servers receive your IP address. This data is not linked to you and is not used by the developer for any other purpose. The Overpass servers and the database service are independent parties with their own policies.

Searching places

If you search for a town or address, your search text and suggestions are handled by Apple Maps. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Routes and distance

For a place, the app shows the travel time and distance by car and on foot. These are calculated by Apple Maps, so your location and the place's location are sent to Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies. The route button opens the route in your device's navigation app.

Searching reviews

The “Search reviews” button opens Google in your browser with a search containing the name, town and country of the place, never your own location. To determine the town and country, the app asks Apple Maps about the location of the place. The app itself sends nothing to Google and does not read back the search results. From the moment Google opens, Google's privacy policy applies.

OpenStreetMap

The place data comes from OpenStreetMap and is licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL). OpenStreetMap contributors maintain that data; the developer of Glutenmaps does not guarantee that it is complete or up to date. If in doubt, or if you have an allergy, always check directly with the venue.

On your device

Saved places, the history of places you viewed and a copy of recently fetched places are stored only on your device. They are not synced or shared. When you delete the app they disappear completely.

Children

The app collects no personal data and is therefore also suitable for use by children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated with the date at the top.
